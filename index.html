Heading elements

Description

H1 to H6 define the HTML headings within a document. They allow for different styles to be assigned to the different heading elements to create different amounts of emphasis visually on the site page. H1 is the most important heading and h6 is the least important heading.

Example of code

<h1>This is heading 1</h1>
<h2>This is heading 2</h2>
<h3>This is heading 3</h3>
<h4>This is heading 4</h4>
<h5>This is heading 5</h5>
<h6>This is heading 6</h6>

Working Example

Quote & Blockquote

Description

A blockquote is a section quoted from another source which is indented on left and right of the page.

A quote is a short quotation which uses "..... " on either side.

Example of code

<blockquote cite="http://www.worldwildlife.org/who/index.html">
For 50 years, WWF has been protecting the future of nature. The world's leading conservation organization, WWF works in 100 countries and is supported by 1.2 million members in the United States and close to 5 million globally.
</blockquote>

<p>WWF's goal is to: <q>Build a future where people live in harmony with nature.</q></p>

 

Working Example

For 50 years, WWF has been protecting the future of nature. The world's leading conservation organization, WWF works in 100 countries and is supported by 1.2 million members in the United States and close to 5 million globally.

WWF's goal is to: Build a future where people live in harmony with nature.

Abbreviations

Description

An abbreviation or an acronym of a long series of words to the first letters of the words.

Example of code

<p>The <abbr title="World Wildlife Fund">WWF</abbr> protects the future of nature.</p>

Working Example

The WWF protects the future of nature.
